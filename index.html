Hackathon guidelines

  • 01
    Build your own team

    Teams should have 2–4 currently enrolled students. Students from different departments may team up.

  • 02
    Start fresh at the event

    Projects should be created during the hackathon. You may use existing libraries and open-source tools; disclose them in your submission.

  • 03
    Choose one project track

    Select the category that best matches your idea and be ready to explain the problem you are solving.

  • 04
    Submit and present as a team

    Submit a working demo or prototype, a short project description, and any source links before the deadline. Every team should be ready to present.

  • 05
    Keep it safe and respectful

    Respect other participants, event staff, and intellectual property. Plagiarism, harassment, and unsafe or unlawful projects are not allowed.

  • 06
    Judging criteria

    Projects will be assessed on usefulness, originality, technical execution, responsible design, and the clarity of the demo.
